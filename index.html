Frequently asked questions

Is the water safe to drink?

Yes. We only onboard lab-tested, certified suppliers, and a supplier is removed after 2 serious complaints.

What if my delivery is late?

Every supplier has a promised delivery window. Late deliveries hurt the supplier's rating, and there is a backup supplier in each area.

How do I pay?

Cash on delivery, or online transfer. The order summary always shows the full split: what the supplier receives and AquaLink's 10% commission.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes — cancel from My orders any time before the order is out for delivery. Weekly plans can be paused or cancelled anytime.

Which areas do you serve?

FB Area, Gulshan, North Karachi and Gulistan-e-Jauhar at launch — about 50,000 households buying bottled water. More areas open as new suppliers join.
